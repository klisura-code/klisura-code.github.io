About Me

Hello, and welcome! I’m an incoming tenure-track Assistant Professor of Information Systems and Security at Kennesaw State University’s Coles College of Business.

I completed my Ph.D. in Information Technology at UT San Antonio, specializing in cybersecurity, under the supervision of Dr. Anthony Rios. My research focuses on trustworthy and secure AI systems, with applications in privacy, access control, and responsible language technologies.

Research

My research builds trustworthy AI systems for sensitive and regulated environments. I study how language models should reason about authorization, confidentiality, fairness, and security, with applications in access control, privacy-policy question answering, and cyber defense.

Confidentiality & Authorization in LLMs

I study how LLMs can fail to protect confidential information or act beyond a user’s permissions. My work develops methods that help language models respect access-control rules, protect private data, and refuse requests when needed.

  • Role-Conditioned Refusals
  • Mechanistic Access Control via Latent Refusal Probing
  • Unmasking Database Vulnerabilities (Text-to-SQL)

Equal Access & Protection

I work to make language technologies safer and more reliable for everyone, including speakers of regional and non-standard dialects. People should receive the same quality, privacy, and protection regardless of how they write or speak.

  • Multi-Agent Dialect-Bias Mitigation
  • Prompt-Probe-Gated Decoding
  • FairPrivacy

AI-Driven Cybersecurity

I use machine learning to detect cyber threats and strengthen defenses, including attacker movement, malware attribution, IoT threats, and early-warning systems built from raw security data.

  • Jbeil (IEEE S&P)
  • IoT Malware Attribution (TDSC)
  • Helium IoT Threat Analysis (WiMob)
