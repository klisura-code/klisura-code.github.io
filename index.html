Research

I build trustworthy AI for sensitive and regulated settings: language models that respect permissions, treat users fairly, and strengthen security. My work spans three connected threads:

Confidentiality & Authorization in LLMs

I study how AI language models can fail at confidentiality and authorization: leaking private information they should protect, or acting beyond what a user is allowed to do. I build models that keep data secret and stay within their permissions.

  • Role-Conditioned Refusals
  • Mechanistic Access Control via Latent Refusal Probing
  • Unmasking Database Vulnerabilities (Text-to-SQL)

Equal Access & Protection

I work to make language technology offer equal access and protection to everyone. People who use regional or non-standard dialects deserve the same quality, privacy, and safety as those who write in "standard" English, no matter how they speak.

  • Multi-Agent Dialect-Bias Mitigation
  • Prompt-Probe-Gated Decoding
  • FairPrivacy

AI-Driven Cybersecurity

I use machine learning to detect cyber threats and strengthen defenses: spotting attackers as they move through a network, tracing malware across the internet, and turning raw security data into early warnings before real damage is done.

  • Jbeil (IEEE S&P)
  • IoT Malware Attribution (TDSC)
  • Helium IoT Threat Analysis (WiMob)
